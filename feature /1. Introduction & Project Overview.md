1. Introduction & Project Overview

Our app, FleetRent, is a vehicle rental and fleet management desktop app built with JavaFX. It is
for small rental shops that rent out cars, SUVs, and motorcycles. We chose Theme Q: Vehicle
Rental & Fleet Management. Many small shops still write their rentals on paper, so it is easy to
lose track of which vehicle is out, how many miles it has driven, and who is next in line. FleetRent
keeps the vehicles, renters, and rentals in one SQLite database, keeps a waiting list when a vehicle
is already out, and lets staff undo the last check-out. Real make and model data comes from the
NHTSA vPIC API, and a dashboard shows the most rented vehicle type and how busy the fleet is
over time.
